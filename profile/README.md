# 🍓 Strawberry Notes

**Handwrite freely. Connect every idea. Learn faster.**

We're building a home for everything you learn. Strawberry Notes brings handwritten notes, flashcards, and tasks together in one app for iPad and iPhone, with AI-powered study tools that work with your notes.

From the first scribble in a lecture to the last review before an exam, keep your ideas and the tools to understand them in one place.

- **Make room for every idea.** Write in an infinite notebook or on an infinite whiteboard with pressure-sensitive pens, pencils, highlighters, and study tape. Annotate PDFs, scan handouts, and bring lecture videos into your notes.
- **See how it all connects.** Link ideas, nest subnotes, and explore Sky View, a map of your notes and their connections. Find what you wrote with on-device handwriting search.
- **Turn notes into understanding.** Ask an AI tutor about your page, or turn your notes into quizzes, study guides, games, and explorable explanations.
- **Remember what matters.** Build flashcards from your notes and review with FSRS spaced repetition. Bring your existing Anki decks along, too.
- **Give every class a home.** Keep notes, flashcards, and tasks together by subject, and share your favorite notes, templates, and decks with other students through the in-app library.

Your library lives on your devices and in your own iCloud Drive. Writing, search, and flashcard reviews work offline. AI is optional, and content is shared with AI providers only with your consent.
